Privacy Policy - Munshi Drive

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Munshi Drive is a personal tool used only by its owner, Dushyant Saini. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

Only photos and captions that the owner himself sends to his own WhatsApp notes bot.

How the data is used

Photos are saved into a folder in the owner's own Google Drive, and a short record (date, time, caption, file reference) is kept in the owner's own Google Sheet. Photos are sent back only to the owner's own WhatsApp number when he asks for them.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or shown to any other person. Access to Google Drive is used only to create and read files in the owner's own Drive.

Deleting data

The owner can delete any saved photo directly from his Google Drive at any time, and can remove this app's access from his Google Account settings.
